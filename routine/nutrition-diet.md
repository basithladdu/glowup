# Nutrition & Caloric Deficit Protocol

## Target Framework
- **Current Weight Baseline:** ~85 kg
- **Calorie Floor:** **2,000 kcal / day** (500 kcal deficit below 2,500 maintenance).
- **Protein Floor:** **170g / day** (non-negotiable for muscle retention and satiety).
- **Target Loss Rate:** **0.4 – 0.6 kg / week** (preserves lean mass while shedding visceral fat).

---

## Daily Meal Structure
1. **Morning Meal (Post-Walk):**
   - 70g Oats + 250ml Milk + 2 tsp ground flaxseed + 1 banana.
   - Creatine Monohydrate 5g (with morning shake or warm water).
2. **Lunch:**
   - 80g Soya Chunks (boiled, squeezed thoroughly to remove bitter isoflavone taste, cooked in light home spices).
   - 150g Cooked Rice + 1 bowl Dal + Sabzi.
   - *Rule:* Eat slowly, chew for a minimum of 15 minutes, chewing on both sides evenly for jaw symmetry.
3. **Midday / Pre-Workout:**
   - Black coffee with L-Theanine (200mg) for clean focus without jitters.
4. **Post-Workout:**
   - 1 Scoop Nakpro Whey (24g protein) in cold water.
5. **Dinner (7:30 PM – 8:30 PM):**
   - 4 Whole Eggs (boiled or scrambled in minimal oil) or 130g Chicken Breast.
   - 2 Roti + 100g fresh curd.
   - Ashwagandha capsule with dinner.

---

## Hydration & Water Weight Management
- **Creatine Notice:** In the first 7–10 days on 5g creatine, muscle cell volumization will cause a 1.0–2.0 kg spike on the scale. This is intracellular water, NOT body fat. Do not adjust calories downwards in response.
- **Daily Fluid Intake:** 3.5 – 4.0 Liters.
