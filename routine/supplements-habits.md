## 2. Posture & Physical Realignment (Daily)
1. **Chin Tucks:** 3 sets of 15 reps against a wall (aligns cervical spine, counters forward-head posture).
2. **Doorway Chest Opener Stretch:** 3 sets of 30 seconds.
3. **Dead Hangs:** 2 sets of 30–45 seconds at the gym or home pullup bar.
4. **Mastication & Jaw Symmetry:** Consciously distribute chewing 50/50 on left and right sides.

- **Content Creation (#basithladdu):** 45–60 minute focused block daily.
