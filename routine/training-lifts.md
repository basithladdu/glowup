# Training & Lifting Protocol: Push Pull Legs (PPL) + Core 4

## Objective & Progression Strategy
- **Primary Goal:** Build V-Taper (Shoulder-to-Waist ratio 1.60), broaden shoulders/upper back, preserve muscle mass during 500 kcal cut.
- **Progression Rule:**
  - **Compound Lifts:** When all prescribed reps are achieved with clean form, add **+2.5 kg** next session.
  - **Accessory Lifts:** Max out the repetition range before increasing load.

---

## Non-Negotiable Core 4 (Every Single Gym Session)

These 4 movements must be executed regardless of push/pull/leg split:
1. **Face Pulls (3 × 15–20 reps):** External shoulder rotation, rear delts, posture correction.
2. **Rear Delt Fly (3 × 15 reps):** Creates upper width and 3D shoulders for the V-taper illusion.
3. **Farmer Carries (3 × 40–50m):** Grip strength, forearms, core stabilization, and traps.
4. **Dead Hangs (2 × 30–45 sec):** Spinal decompression, posture alignment, visible height restoration.

---

## Workout Split

### Push Day
- Incline Dumbbell Bench Press: 3 × 8–10
- Flat Barbell Bench Press: 3 × 6–8
- Standing Overhead DB / BB Press: 3 × 8–10
- Lateral Raises (Strict form): 4 × 12–15
- Cable Tricep Pushdowns / Overhead Extensions: 3 × 12–15
- **Core 4 Accessories** (Face Pulls, Rear Delt, Farmer Carry, Dead Hang)

### Pull Day
- Lat Pulldowns (Wide / Neutral grip): 3 × 8–10
- Chest-Supported T-Bar / DB Row: 3 × 8–10
- Barbell / Cable Rows: 3 × 10–12
- Incline Dumbbell Curls / Hammer Curls: 3 × 10–12
- **Core 4 Accessories** (Face Pulls, Rear Delt, Farmer Carry, Dead Hang)

### Legs & Calves Day
- Barbell Squat or Heavy Leg Press: 3 × 8–10
- Romanian Deadlift (RDL): 3 × 8–10
- Hamstring Leg Curls: 3 × 12–15
- Standing Calf Raises: 4 × 15–20
- **Core 4 Accessories** (Face Pulls, Rear Delt, Farmer Carry, Dead Hang)
