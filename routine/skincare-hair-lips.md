# Skincare, Haircare & Lips Routine

## Daily Core Philosophy
- **Sun protection is 80% of tone correction:** Morning application + midday reapplication closes the face-to-body tone gap.
- **Never over-exfoliate:** Irritation triggers post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), making skin darker instead of even.
- **No Hydroquinone:** High risk of ochronosis on South Asian skin; Azelaic Acid 10% + Kojic + Arbutin provides safe, long-term tyrosinase inhibition.

---

## 1. Morning Protocol (Every Single Day)
1. **Cleanser:** AHA Glow gentle facewash.
2. **Antioxidant Serum:** Pilgrim Vitamin C.
3. **Moisturizer / Barrier Support:** Minimalist Vitamin B12.
4. **Sunscreen (Non-Negotiable):** Lakmé Ultra Matte Lotion SPF 50 (face, neck, ears, arms, and hands).
5. **Lips:** WishCare SPF 50 Lip Balm.
6. **Midday (1:00 PM – 2:00 PM):** Reapply SPF 50.

## 3. Targeted Protocols

### Beard (Tugain 5% Minoxidil & Dermarolling)
- **Mon–Fri & Sat (non-peel):** 1ml Tugain 5% liquid at night.
- **Sunday Morning:** Cleanse skin, 0.5mm dermaroller with gentle cross-hatching. **Wait 24 hours before applying minoxidil.**
- **Grooming:** 1–3mm clean stubble, neckline defined 2 fingers above Adam's apple.

### Lips
- **Daily AM:** WishCare SPF 50 Lip Balm.
- **Tue & Sat PM:** Nicole Lip Scrub (gentle exfoliation).
- **Every Night:** Thick occlusive layer (Vaseline / Aquaphor). Do NOT lick lips.

### Hair & Scalp
- **Ketoconazole 2% Shampoo:** 2× weekly (Tue & Thu) to eliminate dandruff and manage 5-alpha reductase.
- **Redensyl Hair Growth Serum:** Applied directly to scalp every night.
- **Gentle Sulfate-Free Shampoo:** Sunday reset.
