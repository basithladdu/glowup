the cut, honestly

85kg at 5'7" is a real deficit job, not a recomp. ~2,000 kcal, 170g protein floor, 500 under maintenance. As a near-beginner you'll still gain muscle in that deficit — that's the recomp look, just reached faster.

Target rate: 0.4–0.6 kg/week. Faster than that and you're eating muscle. Slower and you'll lose patience.

Weight jumps 1–2kg on creatine in week one. That's water in the muscle, not fat. Don't react to it.

01 priority order

Even out face vs body—

8–12 weeks. SPF 50 morning + midday reapply is 80% of it. Azelaic 3×, kojic 2×, peel every 14 days does the rest. The thing that ruins this is over-peeling — irritation makes you darker, not lighter.

Lighter lips—

6–8 weeks for the dark dehydrated layer, 3–6 months for real change. SPF lip balm every day (sun darkens lips — most people never know this), scrub Tue + Sat, occlusive nightly, stop licking them. Honest limit: if your natural pigment is dark you'll get healthier and less dark, not pink.

Big back & V-taper—

First changes 6–8 weeks, real taper 3–6 months. Shoulder:waist 1.6 is the number. Built from two ends — lats and rear/side delts up, waist down. Rear delt fly and face pulls every session are what most people skip and then wonder why they look narrow.

Beard density—

Shedding weeks 2–4 (normal, don't panic). First real growth 3–4 months. Fill 9–12 months. Tugain 5% Mon–Fri, dermaroll Sunday, minoxidil 24h after rolling — never the same day. Meanwhile the "clean but could grow it" look is grooming, not growth: 1–3mm stubble, sharp neckline two fingers above the Adam's apple.

Lean—

0.4–0.6 kg/week. Leanness is also the quiet lever on three of the goals above — it sharpens the jaw, makes asymmetry less readable, and makes the taper visible. It's not a separate project.

02 what won't move

Saying this once so you don't waste money on it later.

Facial asymmetry is bone. Exercises and mewing won't restructure it. What actually reduces how visible it is: lower body fat, even chewing, posture, and framing with hair + stubble. Manage the expectation, run the free habits, skip anything sold as a fix.

You won't grow taller at 22. Growth plates are fused. But the "compressed when I wake up" feeling is real — dead hangs, chin tucks and chest opening recover 1–3cm of visible height you're losing to slouching. That's a genuine win, just not the one people advertise.

Pores don't close permanently. They look 40–60% smaller with consistent exfoliation and SPF. Anything promising to shrink them is lying.

Hydroquinone is off the list. It's a real drug for patches, not full-face tan, and on Indian skin unsupervised use risks ochronosis — permanent blue-black darkening. Azelaic 10% does the job you actually want with none of that. If 12 weeks of the current stack doesn't close the gap, that's a dermatologist conversation, not a self-prescription.
